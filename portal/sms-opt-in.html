How subcontractors opt in to text messages

Six Arrows Construction · Last updated August 2026

This page documents how Six Arrows Construction collects consent before sending scheduling text messages. It is published so that carriers, subcontractors and anyone reviewing our messaging program can see exactly how consent is obtained.

Who receives messages

Only subcontractors we have hired for a job. We do not message consumers, we do not send marketing or promotional messages, and we do not buy, rent or import phone number lists. Numbers are never collected from our website or from any online form.

Where and when consent is collected

  1. Consent is collected during subcontractor onboarding, in person or by phone, at the point a subcontractor is first engaged for a job and before any work order is sent.
  2. Onboarding is when we also collect the subcontractor's W-9 and certificate of insurance.
  3. The subcontractor completes a Subcontractor Information Form recording business name, contact name, trade and mobile number. That form contains the consent block below, which the subcontractor signs and dates.
  4. When a number is taken over the phone, the coordinator reads the same wording aloud verbatim and the form is then sent for signature.
  5. A mobile number is not added to our scheduling system until the signed form is returned. Signed forms are retained in our project files.

The exact consent wording

"I authorize Six Arrows Construction to send text messages to the mobile number above about scheduling and work orders for jobs I am assigned, including links to work orders and follow-up reminders when a response has not been received.

Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per project. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of being hired or of any purchase.

What the messages contain

A link to a work order showing the scope, dates and payment terms for that subcontractor's work, and reminders when we have not heard back. Every message identifies Six Arrows Construction.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message from our number at any time. See our SMS Terms and Conditions for full details, and our Privacy Policy for how we handle this information.
