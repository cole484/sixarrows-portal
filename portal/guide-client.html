Portal Guide

Welcome to your Six Arrows Blueprint Portal. This guide will help you navigate every feature and get the most out of your project experience.

Logging In

Your portal is your private window into your home-building project. Here's how to access it:

1Visit the portal link provided by your Six Arrows team
You'll see the Blueprint Portal welcome page with our four pillars: Clarity, Momentum, Transparency, and Communication.
2Enter your email and access code
Use the email address and access code that your project team provided. These are unique to your project.
3You're in!
You'll see a "Welcome back" message and be taken directly to your Command Center.
Tip: Bookmark the portal link on your phone and computer for easy access. You'll stay logged in until you close the browser tab or clear your browsing data.
Lost your access code? Contact your Six Arrows team — they can look it up or reset it for you.

Your Command Center

The Command Center is your home base — it gives you a snapshot of everything happening with your project at a glance. What you see depends on where you are in the process:

During Preconstruction (SAB Phase)

  • Current Step — A highlighted card showing exactly where you are in the process, what's happening now, and who's responsible (Six Arrows, you, or both)
  • Build Readiness — An overall progress bar showing how close you are to breaking ground
  • SAB Tracker — Quick link to your detailed step-by-step progress
  • Selections — How many material and finish selections you've completed
  • Groundbreaking Countdown — A live countdown to your groundbreaking date (once set)
  • Open Decisions — Any items that need your input, with urgency labels
  • Recent Photos — Latest project photos

During Construction

  • Move-In Countdown — Days remaining until your target completion date
  • Active Phase — Current construction phase and completion percentage
  • Timeline Progress — Days since start, overall completion, and next milestone
  • Budget Overview — How much of your budget has been committed
  • Active Milestones — What's been completed and what's coming next
  • Open Decisions — Any items still needing your input

SAB Tracker

The SAB Tracker is your roadmap through the preconstruction process. It breaks everything down into clear phases and steps so you always know where things stand.

Three Phases

  1. Phase 1: Design — Architectural plans, engineering, HOA review, and permitting. This is where your home takes shape on paper.
  2. Phase 2: Selections — Choosing all of your materials, finishes, and fixtures. This is where you make it yours.
  3. Phase 3: Build-Ready — Final budget review, pre-construction meeting, and preparation for groundbreaking.

How It Works

  • Each phase has multiple steps, and each step has checklist items that get completed along the way
  • Steps show who's responsible — "Six Arrows is handling this", "Your decision required", or "Collaborative"
  • You can switch between phase tabs to see what's ahead — all phases are visible so you can plan accordingly
  • When you open the tracker, it automatically takes you to your current active phase
  • The progress bar at the top shows your overall Build Readiness percentage
Tip: Check the tracker regularly to stay on top of what's happening and what's coming next. If a step shows "Your decision required," your input is needed to keep things moving.

Making Selections

One of the most exciting parts of building your home is choosing your finishes and materials. The Selections App walks you through everything in three organized waves.

The Three Waves

WaveWhat You'll ChooseWhy This Order
Wave 1: Structure Fireplace, Appliances, Exterior Finishes These affect the structural design and need to be decided early
Wave 2: The Shell Specialty Trim, Flooring, Tile These are installed during the framing and rough-in phases
Wave 3: The Jewelry Plumbing, Lighting, Countertops, Hardware, Paint, Interior Doors & Trim The finishing touches that bring your home to life

How to Use the Selections App

1Open the Selections App
From the Selections page or Command Center, click "Open Selections App".
2Set up your rooms (first time only)
The first time you open the app, you'll configure which rooms your home has and what fixtures each room needs (sinks, toilets, etc.). This customizes the selections to your floor plan.
3Work through each category
Select a wave tab, then click through each category (Appliances, Flooring, etc.). Fill in your choices — product names, colors, SKUs, and any notes. Green guidance boxes offer helpful tips along the way.
4Save as you go
Your selections save automatically. You can close the app and come back anytime — your progress is preserved across devices.
5Skip categories that don't apply
If a category doesn't apply to your project (e.g., no fireplace), you can skip it and it will be marked as complete.
Tip: You don't have to finish everything in one sitting. The app saves your progress, so you can work through it at your own pace. Many clients find it helpful to tackle one wave at a time.

Design Book

The Design Book is a beautiful visual reference that compiles all of your selections into a room-by-room lookbook. Think of it as your home's style guide.

What's Inside

  • Cover page with your project name
  • Room-by-room spreads — Kitchen, bathrooms, living areas, and more
  • Product cards showing images, names, colors, and specifications for every selection
  • Categories covered: Flooring, tile, countertops, paint, doors and trim, plumbing fixtures, lighting, appliances, and more

How to Access It

You'll find a "View Design Book" link on your Command Center's selections card. You can also reach it from the Selections page.

Printing & Sharing

The Design Book has a Print button in the top bar — it's formatted for clean printing on paper. You can also use your browser's "Save as PDF" option to create a digital copy to share with family or your design team.

Tip: The Design Book updates automatically as you complete selections. Check back after finishing a new wave to see your latest choices beautifully presented.

Viewing Your Budget

The Budget page gives you full transparency into your project's financials.

During Preconstruction

You'll see your budget categories and their status as selections are finalized. Each category shows whether it's:

  • Complete — Budget for this category is locked in
  • In Review — Being finalized with your Six Arrows team
  • Pending — Still awaiting selections or pricing

During Construction

The budget view expands to include:

  • Budget Health — A visual indicator showing how much of your total budget has been committed
  • Category Breakdown — Each category with target amount, current spending, and variance
  • Change Orders — Any budget modifications made during construction, with descriptions and amounts
  • Forecast — Projected total including pending change orders
Tip: Budget data syncs from your project's master spreadsheet, so the numbers you see are always current.

Project Timeline

The Timeline page shows your project journey from start to finish.

During Preconstruction

You'll see a visual journey through the three SAB phases, showing:

  • Which phases are complete (green checkmark), in progress (highlighted), or upcoming (gray)
  • Step progress within each phase
  • Target dates and milestone dates
  • Your groundbreaking date countdown

During Construction

The timeline shows your build schedule with:

  • Recently completed milestones
  • The current active task
  • Upcoming tasks on the schedule
  • Days since start and days remaining

Project Updates

The Updates page is where your Six Arrows team shares news about your project. You'll find:

  • Regular updates — Posted throughout the week with progress reports, next steps, and important notes
  • Date stamps — Each update is dated so you can track the timeline
  • Detailed content — Updates include descriptions of work completed, upcoming activities, and anything that needs your attention

Updates also appear as a summary on your Command Center, so you'll see the latest news every time you log in.

Tip: Check the Updates page regularly — it's the best way to stay informed about day-to-day progress on your project.

Decisions

Throughout your project, there will be moments where your input is needed. The Decisions section keeps track of all outstanding items that require your attention.

Each decision shows:

  • Title — What needs to be decided (e.g., "Confirm kitchen backsplash tile")
  • Status — Urgency level:
    • Urgent — Needs your response soon to avoid delays
    • Active — On the table, but no immediate deadline
    • Queued — Coming up, but not needed right away
  • Notes — Additional context or details from your team

Open decisions also appear on your Command Center with an alert badge, so you'll always know when something needs your attention.

Important: Responding promptly to urgent decisions helps keep your project on schedule. If you have questions about a decision, reach out to your project team.

Your Project Team

Your project team's contact information is available on the Updates page. You'll find:

  • Customer Experience contact — Your primary point of contact for questions, scheduling, and general communication
  • Project Manager — Handles the day-to-day build coordination and technical details

Each team member's card shows their name, title, phone number (tap to call on mobile), and email address (tap to email). Don't hesitate to reach out with questions — that's what they're there for.

Documents

The Documents page is your central file cabinet for all project-related files. Documents are organized by category:

  • Architectural Plans — Floor plans, elevations, and design drawings
  • Budget & Financials — Budget sheets and financial documents
  • Selections — Selection sheets and specifications
  • Contracts — Signed agreements and contracts
  • Permits — Building permits and approvals

Viewing Documents

Click on any document to open it. PDFs can be previewed directly in the portal — no need to download. Other file types (spreadsheets, images) open in a new tab.

Use the filter pills at the top to narrow down by category if you're looking for something specific.

Build Photos

The Photos section (found under the Documents page) shows progress photos from your build, organized by construction phase.

Browsing Photos

  • Photos are grouped by phase — Foundation, Framing, Mechanical, Drywall, Finishes, etc.
  • Click any photo thumbnail to open a full-screen lightbox
  • Use the arrow buttons (or swipe on mobile) to navigate between photos
  • Photos are added by your Six Arrows team as the build progresses

Inspiration Board

The "Your Inspiration" tab lets you save inspiration images that you've collected. Add images and label them to share your vision with your build team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I access the portal from my phone?

Yes! The portal is fully responsive and works great on phones, tablets, and computers. The navigation adapts to your screen — you'll see a bottom tab bar on mobile instead of the sidebar.

Do I need to install an app?

No — the portal runs in your web browser. No app download needed. Just visit the link and log in.

Will I lose my progress if I close the browser?

No. Your selections, progress, and all project data are saved to our servers. You can close the browser, switch devices, and pick up right where you left off.

How often is the portal updated?

Your project data updates in real-time. Budget numbers, timeline milestones, and tracker progress sync whenever you load a page. Your Six Arrows team posts updates regularly throughout the week.

Can my spouse or partner also access the portal?

Yes — you share the same login credentials, so anyone with your email and access code can log in. You can be logged in on multiple devices at the same time.

I made a mistake in my selections — can I change them?

Yes. You can go back into the Selections App and update any choices at any time before they're finalized. If a selection has already been locked in, reach out to your project team to discuss changes.

What do the urgency labels on decisions mean?

  • Urgent — This decision is time-sensitive and could affect your schedule if delayed
  • Active — Needs your input, but there's some flexibility on timing
  • Queued — Coming up soon, giving you a heads-up to start thinking about it
