Privacy Policy

Six Arrows Construction · Last updated August 2026

This policy explains what information Six Arrows Construction collects from the subcontractors and clients we work with, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information we collect

  • Contact information — name, mobile phone number, email address, and business name, provided to us directly when you begin working with us.
  • Project information — the scope, dates, location and payment terms of work orders assigned to you.
  • Message records — the scheduling messages we send you and your replies, including opt-out requests.

How we use it

  • To send scheduling and work order messages by text message and email.
  • To follow up when we have not received a response about scheduling.
  • To keep records of work assigned, agreed and completed.
  • To meet our own legal, tax and insurance record-keeping obligations.

Text messaging and your mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors and service providers strictly for the purpose of delivering our own messages — for example, our SMS delivery provider — is excluded from this restriction. We do not sell your information.

Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per project. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of being hired or of any purchase.

See how subcontractors opt in for the exact consent wording we use.

Text message consent is not shared with any third party, and your mobile number is never provided to anyone for their own marketing use.

See our SMS Terms and Conditions for how to stop receiving messages.

Who we share information with

We share information only where it is needed to do the work or run the business:

  • Service providers who operate systems on our behalf, such as our text message delivery provider, email provider and file storage — and only to the extent needed to provide that service to us.
  • Clients and other trades on the same project, where scheduling requires it — for example, letting a general contractor know which subcontractor is on site on which day.
  • Authorities or advisors, where we are required to by law or need to establish or defend a legal claim.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own advertising or marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep project and contact records for as long as we work with you and afterwards for as long as our tax, insurance and legal obligations require. Opt-out records are kept indefinitely, because we need them to make sure we do not message you again.

Your choices

  • You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us.
  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it.
  • You can ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.

Use the contact details below for any of these requests.

Security

We limit access to your information to the people at Six Arrows Construction who need it, and we use reputable service providers with their own security practices. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services are for businesses and the people who work with us. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
